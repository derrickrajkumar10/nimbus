What changed from the first Deep tide

  • Real map. MapLibre with OpenFreeMap tiles, recoloured to the app's sea palette. The Segments, pins and popup come straight from contracts/fixtures/.
  • Solid panels, no glass. Docked, opaque surfaces with hairline borders. Nothing floats over the data except small map controls.
  • Colour has a job. Teal means an action the officer can take. Amber and red mean predicted risk. Cyan means water a Reading confirmed. Everything else stays navy and white.
  • No decoration. No gradients, glows, avatars or icons for their own sake. The only wave is the storm's rain curve, and it is data.
  • One way in for everything. Click a Segment, pin, SOS or Resource, on the map or in a list, and the map flies to it and opens the same inspector.
  • Keyboard first. A approves the selected Decision, / asks the Commander. Approving updates the card and the feed in place.
